Set up a sit

Lie on your back and rest the phone flat on your belly, just below the ribs, screen up. Turn off silent mode so you can hear the chimes.

Finding your breath

Breathe normally for half a minute. Don't change anything; the sensor only needs to see a few ordinary breaths.

How to play

  • TapOne breath. Count one to nine in your head.
  • Double-tapThe tenth breath. Then start again at one.
  • Press & holdYou noticed you'd wandered. Let it go and start again at one.

Every few minutes a soft chime asks where you were. Tap if you were with your breath. Press and hold if you were somewhere else. The only setback is a chime that finds you away before you noticed.

Breaths-to-catch is how many breaths passed between losing the count and noticing. Lower means you're catching drift sooner. Zero means you caught it before the count slipped.

Attention test (SART)

Digits from 1 to 9 flash one at a time. Tap for every digit except 3. When you see a 3, don't tap.

The test is independent of the sit, which is the point: if breaths-to-catch tracks real attention, your mistakes on 3 should fall as your sits improve. In the planned study it's given separately from the app; here it sits alongside for convenience.

Your result

Taps on a 3 measure lapses of attention, the main SART score. Missed digits and response time are context.
