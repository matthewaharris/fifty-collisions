    Rules
    • Each turn, do one thing: move, buy an enemy piece you attack, or reprice up to the limit of your own pieces (prices 1–12).
    • A bought piece flips to the buyer where it stands and keeps its price. The buyer pays the owner. It can't be bought straight back on the next ply.
    • Kings are never for sale. Buying the piece that gives check gets you out of check. Repricing never does.
    • After every Black move: each side pays the tax rate times its posted prices, collects rent for pieces on the four centre squares, and pays a progressive tax on a large treasury.
    • From round 30 the tax rate climbs each round, so long games end.
    • Below zero coins after settlement, you are bankrupt and lose. Both at once is a draw. Checkmate still wins. Ply 200 is a draw.
    • 6×6 Los Alamos board: no bishops, no castling, no double pawn step. Pawns promote to queen, rook or knight, priced at 9, 5 or 3.
