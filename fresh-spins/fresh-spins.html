Two-agent brainstorm · Visionary × Builder · 6 turns per idea

Fresh Spins

Eight ideas we could build and sell within a year, each a fresh spin on something people already buy or put up with: a dishwasher magnet, a door notice, a tip log, an estimate pad. The same two agents behind Fifty Collisions worked each one through six turns, grounded in market research from October 2026. Round one allowed any product; round two was apps only, with the phone or watch as the device.

Three scores, 1–10

InnovationSame scale as Fifty Collisions. Target 3–6: a real, nameable improvement, not a new category.
FeasibilityTarget 7+: a team of 1–4 ships a sellable version in 90 days.
Market fitPaying, returning customers within 12 months. Target 7+. A 5 means plausible but unproven.
Anchor: OACP / liteneen.net scores 3 on innovation. Exactly one idea, Counter, is built on it.

Where to start

Ranked by the shape of the business and the cost of finding out, not by score alone.

Method: the moderator wrote one seed per idea, each naming an old product and pointing at mechanisms in Fifty Collisions to borrow. Two Claude agents then ran a fixed six-turn loop: pitch, ground, sharpen, stress-test, converge, record. Round one (ideas 1–5) used one agent pair, and round two (ideas 6–8 and the Morning Tile app supplement) a fresh pair. Both worked from two researched briefs: one on products and channels, one on apps.
Scores are the Builder's calibrated judgment, not market validation. Figures marked unverified in the transcripts are unverified.
