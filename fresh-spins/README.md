# Fresh Spins

Five near-term product ideas: fresh spins on old, boring products that a small team could build in 90 days and sell within 12 months. The same two agents as the original fifty (a **Visionary** and a **Builder**) worked each idea through six turns, retuned for this brief: pitch, ground, sharpen, stress-test, converge, record. They were told to borrow mechanisms from the fifty, ground every claim in a new [market brief](research/market_brief.md), and keep OACP to exactly one idea.

## The five

| # | Idea | Old product | The one change | First buyer | I / F / M |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | [Ran It](transcripts/idea-01.md) | Clean/dirty dishwasher magnet | A clip that flips itself to CLEAN after a real wash (a thermal mass ignores a hot-tap rinse) | Households; then short-term-rental hosts in 10-packs | 3 / 8 / 5 |
| 02 | [Posted](transcripts/idea-02.md) | Paper notice on the door | Each posting is photographed and sealed per door, and the tenant gets the identical record plus a rights page | California and HUD-assisted property managers, per legal notice | 4 / 7 / 5 |
| 03 | [Morning Tile](transcripts/idea-03.md) | Fridge calendar and the "call Mum" routine | She drops a tile and presses its number; that unlocks the grandkids' voice note of the day | Adult children of parents living alone | 4 / 8 / 6 |
| 04 | [Counter](transcripts/idea-04.md) **(OACP)** | Price list taped by the phone | Ranges built from the clinic's own invoices, signed once, quoted identically to callers, Google and agents | Independent vet clinics | 4 / 7 / 5 |
| 05 | [Afterlist](transcripts/idea-05.md) | Shoebox of receipts and the home-inventory app | Rebuild the contents list after a loss from order histories and statements, with an evidence tier on every line | Public adjusters on unwaived total losses | 4 / 7 / 5 |

I / F / M = innovation, feasibility, and market fit within 12 months (1–10). Innovation uses the original scale, with OACP at 3. Full records are in [`records/`](records/), one-line summaries in [`catalog.md`](catalog.md), and the rules in [`PROTOCOL.md`](PROTOCOL.md) and [`seeds.md`](seeds.md).

## An honest read on market fit

The brief targeted market fit of 7 or more. None of the five got there on paper: four scored 5 and Morning Tile 6. That isn't for lack of trying. The moderator pushed on it every round, and both agents refused to score demand they hadn't seen. Each idea names the single result that would move it to 6 or 7, and each has a demand test that costs about $1,500 or a few weeks of founder time. Every test can run before any real code, and all five could run in parallel in a month.

| Idea | Demand test | Go if | Kill if |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ran It | 15 hand-built clips, silent 5-second videos, $600 of ads to a refundable pre-order page (3 weeks) | 1.5%+ of visitors pre-order at under $8 per customer | Eco cycles missed more than 2% of the time, or under 8 orders a day 60 days after launch |
| Posted | Free 3-week concierge pilots with 3 operators | 2 of 3 use it on 80%+ of legal notices and 1 pays $6 a notice | Under 8 paying operators or 400 paid notices a month by month 6 |
| Morning Tile | Meta ads to a $12/month card-on-file pre-sale; 30 families on a printed board and phone line (3 weeks) | $60 or less per customer, and 70%+ of parents calling 5 of 7 days in week 3 | Under 60% calling by day 60, or churn over 8% a month |
| Counter | Desk audit of 200 clinics' visible prices, sent as a free report with a $49 offer, plus 40 founder calls (3 weeks) | 10+ clinics pay | Under 40 paying clinics by month 6. The OACP part has its own kill: under 50 agent quote requests in 90 days |
| Afterlist | Free 72-hour concierge rebuild of one live file for 60 public adjusters (4 weeks) | 5+ adjusters send a second file at $199, and 50%+ of claim value lands in the proven tiers | Under 15 paying firms by month 6, or desk adjusters accepting order-backed lines less than 70% of the time |

## Recommended order

1. **Morning Tile.** It's the best-shaped business: monthly revenue, a payer who already spends on pendants and paid for Amazon's discontinued Alexa Together, and a first version made of a phone line and a printer. The risk is retention, and the test measures it directly.
2. **Afterlist** and **Posted** together. Both are B2B tests that cost founder time rather than money, both sell evidence to someone with a legal or financial deadline, and both share the same core (sealed records with a clear evidence standard).
3. **Ran It.** It's the cheapest physical test and a good TikTok product, but it's an impulse buy with no consumable. Run it only if the short-term-rental 10-pack shows reorders.
4. **Counter**, the OACP idea. The price-list product stands on its own. The OACP lane is honest upside with a hard kill while Google's assistant still phones businesses instead of speaking a protocol. Build OACP's signed-quote path as a small extension of the published list, not as the pitch.

## Themes worth noting

- **Evidence was the hidden innovation in three of five.** Posted (sealed postings), Counter (signed, invoice-backed quotes) and Afterlist (per-line evidence tiers) each turned a boring artifact into something two opposing parties can both trust. That's the mild, sellable descendant of several of the fifty (Piepowder, Settlement, Recount).
- **The agents cut their own best-sounding features** when they failed on ethics or law: logging when tenants open a notice, outbound calls to elderly parents, AI test calls to clinics, and a consumer concierge that would count as unlicensed adjusting.
- **Physical products struggled on market fit.** Without a consumable or a visible colourway, a clever object is a one-time sale that copycats undercut within months.
