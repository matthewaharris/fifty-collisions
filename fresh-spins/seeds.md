# Fresh Spins: the 5 seeds

Each seed names an old product, says why it might be ripe, and points at mechanisms in the fifty (`../catalog.md`) that could be borrowed. Seeds are springboards: go somewhere better if the old product points there, but keep the brief in PROTOCOL.md. Exactly one seed (04) is about OACP.

01. **A household object people own and mildly resent** (physical product; Amazon and TikTok Shop lane). The kitchen timer, the reusable ice pack, the dish rack, the lunchbox, the key hook, the doorstop, the laundry basket. Pick one with a large installed base and a daily annoyance. Possible borrowings: #9 Caught (a single control that only registers noticing), #38 Tally (a tag board that proves everyone is out), #11 Washout (a tiny self-experiment built into a habit). Constraint: tooling and first run under about $50K, retail price $15–60, a demo that works in a 5-second video.

02. **The construction notice taped to the lobby door** (SMB software or service for small contractors and property managers). The paper notice and the door hanger that warn neighbours about noise, water shut-offs and parking. Possible borrowings: #35 Lookahead (a noise forecast the contractor commits to and is held to), #14 Second Shift (who's on site and when). Who pays, and why they'd pay this year, matters more than the tech.

03. **The fridge calendar and the "call Mum" routine** (caregiving for ageing parents; app, device or service). Families check on parents through calls, texts, shared calendars and the occasional camera. Possible borrowings: #20 Facing Out (warning signs that face out only to chosen people, through scarce, graded clues), #38 Tally (a nightly check that proves the system still works), #31 Tombstone (map what depends on what before a crisis). Respect the parent's dignity and stay clear of medical-device claims.

04. **OACP: the out-of-office reply and the receptionist's message pad** (agent-facing front desk for small businesses). Customers' AI agents now email, call and fill forms to book, ask and buy. Small businesses have no front desk for agents. Possible borrowings: OACP itself (signed identities, capability discovery, store-and-forward messaging), #28 Tab (spending limits an agent carries and a server verifies offline), #45 Repro (make the caller prove something before a human reads it), #47 Piepowder (a record of what the agent was told). This is the only OACP seed.

05. **The shoebox of receipts, warranty cards and owner's manuals** (personal or household admin; app, service or hybrid). Every home has a drawer of paper that matters exactly once: a warranty claim, a recall, an insurance claim after a burglary, a resale. Possible borrowings: #31 Tombstone (map what recovers what), #22 Unaccounted (product passports scanned from the object itself; the EU Digital Product Passport rollout), #36 Settlement (seal what you expected, check it later).

## Round 2: apps only (ideas 06–08)

06. **The tip log and the mileage notebook** (consumer app for tipped and gig workers). Servers, bartenders, stylists, delivery and rideshare drivers track cash tips and miles on paper, in notes apps, or not at all. The 2025 tax law's "no tax on tips" deduction (up to $25k a year for 2025–2028, for listed occupations) and the higher 1099 thresholds mean more people must keep their own records, and the 2027 filing season is the deadline. Possible borrowings: Afterlist's evidence tiers (idea 05), #11 Washout (records you can't fool by peeking), #36 Settlement (seal it at the time). Stay clear of tax-advice claims and lending.

07. **The paper blood-pressure log** (consumer health app on the phone and watch). Doctors ask patients to keep a week of home readings in a notebook, and the Apple Watch now flags possible hypertension, sending millions of people to buy a cuff and start logging. Possible borrowings: #11 Washout (a self-experiment you can't fool: did cutting salt or walking after dinner actually move your numbers?), #20 Facing Out (share with the people you choose), Morning Tile's ritual design. No diagnostic claims; logging, reminders and a doctor-ready report are the line.

08. **The carbon-copy job ticket and the estimate pad** (SMB app for solo tradespeople and small crews, run from a phone). Plumbers, electricians, painters, handymen and remodelers still quote on paper or in text messages, and disputes over scope and change orders cost them money. Jobber and Housecall Pro dominate scheduling and invoicing, so find the job they don't do well. Possible borrowings: #35 Lookahead (a forecast the contractor commits to), Posted's sealed records (idea 02), Counter's ranges from your own invoices (idea 04).
