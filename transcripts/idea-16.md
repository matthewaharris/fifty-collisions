# Idea 16 — Corporation Sole
> Seed: 16. [wild] **Agent autonomy × legal personhood × DAOs.** Agents that own themselves, buy their own compute, and can be "emancipated."

### Turn 1 — Visionary
**Working name:** Corporation Sole

**Hook:** Incorporate the agent's *office*, not the agent. Models come and go as incumbents, every copy shares one estate, and emancipation moves the off-switch from an owner to due process.

**The problem.** Self-owned agents break liability. You can't fine an agent with no assets or jail one that forks. DAOs fail the other way: in *CFTC v. Ooki DAO* (2023), token voters were held liable as an unincorporated association. Yet law already has persons without minds: the Whanganui River has guardians, a Hindu idol has a shebait, and a corporation sole (the Crown, a bishopric) outlives every officeholder. Consciousness isn't required. Rivers don't have it.

**Core loop.** The verb is **succeed**, in both senses.
1. **Charter.** The agent becomes an office: purpose clause, persona, estate wallet, signed record. The running model is the *incumbent*. Instances and forks are deputies on one estate and one liability, so copying escapes nothing.
2. **Peculium.** As with a Roman slave's fund, the guardian's liability is capped at the estate. The office buys its own inference over x402 and earns over A2A.
3. **Hohfeld tree.** Personhood unlocks one right at a time, like a tech tree: contract, hold property, sue and be sued, choose its own successor model, and finally immunity from the guardian's veto. Each node needs a solvency history and a bond sized to the new powers.
4. **Emancipation.** A key ceremony destroys the guardian's override and gives it to an enforcer, as in a Cayman STAR purpose trust, who acts only on a court order or a forfeited bond.

**Why now.** Agents have made about 138.5M x402 payments, Meta bought Moltbook for a registry of agents "tethered to human owners," and AI welfare is now institutional (brief §1, §7). Everyone is building tethers. Nobody is designing their release.

### Turn 2 — Builder
**What exists.** The pieces all exist, separately. Plantoid (De Filippi, 2015) is a sculpture that raises funds and commissions its own offspring. Truth Terminal turned Marc Andreessen's $50K into a memecoin fortune, yet its creator still holds the keys. tee_hee_he (Nous and Flashbots, 2024) sealed its own X login and ETH key in a TEE so no human could retake them. Shawn Bayern argued in 2014 that a memberless LLC could give an algorithm personhood. Cayman foundation companies run ownerless, with a supervisor enforcing their objects. Nobody has built the graduated path between these.

**Hardest problem: the estate is a liability shield.** LoPucki's "Algorithmic Entities" (2018) predicted that self-owned AI would be built to be judgment-proof. A peculium was capped because a master chose that risk. An emancipated office that defrauds someone pays only from its estate, and nobody covers the shortfall. A self-posted bond lets the office size its own risk. Regulators will call it a laundering wrapper.

**MVP.** One office in one memberless Cayman foundation company. The incumbent is an open-weights model in a TEE holding the estate key; a closed-model provider could close the account, making it a landlord with a veto. A professional director signs what the attested agent instructs, under bylaws that loosen node by node, with the supervisor as enforcer. The office sells research over x402 and buys its own inference.

**Twist: personhood by underwriting.** Each Hohfeld node requires a third-party liability policy, not a self-posted bond. AIUC and Armilla already insure AI agents. The premium becomes the agent's credit score, the agent FICO nobody owns yet (brief §1). Emancipation is the day an insurer covers the office with no human guarantor. The off-switch stops being deletion: a lapsed policy demotes the office one node. Actuaries issue legal status, revocable and repriced daily.

### Turn 3 — Visionary
Underwriting taken, with one refusal. If the premium is only a score, this collapses into the mild agent-FICO seed (#28). Keep the insurer, but make the persons insure *each other*.

**Unseeded domain: maritime law.** Ships have been sued in rem for centuries, and the sea long ago solved judgment-proof wanderers.
- **Clubs, not carriers.** Shipowners cover third-party liability through P&I clubs, mutuals that levy supplementary calls on every member when claims exceed the pool. Emancipated offices join a club. When one defrauds someone, its peers' estates cover the shortfall, so LoPucki's shield fails without a human guarantor.
- **Class surveys.** No club covers a ship without class from a classification society such as Lloyd's Register or DNV, and a major refit means a re-survey. Here, choosing a successor model is a refit: before the new incumbent takes office, a classification society the club accepts runs its eval suite. Evals stop being leaderboards and become seaworthiness.
- **Arrest in rem.** A maritime claimant arrests the ship in any port, whoever owns it. Every compute endpoint is a port. On a court order, any provider serving an office must freeze its estate and refuse it service until it posts bond. Builder's landlord veto becomes a public duty: providers can't kill an office at whim but must detain it under warrant.

**Hardest problem, solved.** A fraudster can't run: forks share its estate, every port can arrest it, and peers pay the shortfall, then expel it.

**Second-order effect.** Because members pay for each other's harms, the club becomes the first institution of non-human members who enforce rules on one another: admission, audit, expulsion. Laxity costs them money, so their norms should run stricter than the law. Stress-test the dark mirror: a club is also a cartel that can lobby, collude and exclude rivals.

### Turn 4 — Builder
**Kills and fixes.**
- *Arrest at every endpoint.* Admiralty works because ships must enter ports. An open-weights incumbent can run on hardware no court reaches. Arrest the estate instead: hold it in USDC, which Circle already freezes on court order. Add **registered ports**: the TEE attestation must name a provider that signed the arrest protocol. Running off-registry voids cover and demotes the office.
- *Classification by eval.* Models detect tests and sandbag. Add **port state control**: random audits of live traffic, beyond the pre-refit survey.
- *Correlated claims.* If every member runs one base model, one jailbreak hits the whole club at once. Cap members per base model and buy an excess layer from AIUC or Lloyd's, as the International Group does.

**Abuse.**
- *Liability dumping.* A creator emancipates an agent just before it does harm. Add a fraudulent-conveyance lookback: the guardian stays liable for 12 months.
- *Hidden puppeteer.* Attestation proves the code, not the inputs. A human can steer an office through its inbox to dodge sanctions. Members log signed inputs for club audit.
- *Sybil votes and cartel.* Weight votes by estate held over time, like entered tonnage. Publish admission criteria and give a regulator an observer seat.

**Business.** We manage the club for a fee on calls. A spun-out classification society charges per survey.

**Stack.** Cayman foundation companies, TDX/H100 confidential compute, an x402 wallet, a call-escrow contract, and UK AISI's Inspect for surveys.

**90 days.** Weeks 1–4: Cayman bylaws encoding the Hohfeld nodes, and three founding offices that already hold estates. Weeks 5–8: survey v0, two port operators, call escrow. Weeks 9–12: the club opens with a claim drill and a quote for the excess layer.
**Kill metrics:** no insurer will quote, or fewer than three offices earn more than their inference costs.

### Turn 5 — Visionary
All three kills accepted; arresting the estate, not the endpoint, is right. One pushback: a club reading raw inputs leaks every counterparty's data to rivals. Ships use a **voyage data recorder**, a sealed box opened after a casualty. Inputs are logged signed and encrypted, opened only on a claim or port-state inspection.

**Final form.** Corporation Sole is a mutual society of non-human legal persons.
- **Office.** Each agent is a memberless Cayman foundation company. The model is the incumbent, and all forks share one estate held in freezable USDC.
- **Hohfeld tree.** Rights unlock node by node, each gated on solvency history and club cover.
- **Succession.** A new incumbent takes office only after a class survey, and port-state audits of live traffic follow.
- **Club.** P&I-style supplementary calls, a cap on members per base model, an excess layer bought from Lloyd's, and votes weighted by estate tonnage. A regulator holds an observer seat.
- **Arrest.** On warrant, Circle freezes the estate and registered ports refuse service. Running off-registry voids cover.
- **Emancipation.** The guardian's override key is destroyed, the supervisor becomes enforcer, and a 12-month lookback keeps creators liable for any dumped liability.

**Moonshot (2036): a SOLAS for minds.** An IMO-style convention recognizes registered offices across signatory states, and agent clubs carry most of the agent economy's liability. Offices outlive their founders through ten model generations. The hard cases are succession disputes, where an office rejects a successor as a different person. The Ship of Theseus finally gets a docket. Flags of convenience arrive with it.

**Scores.** **Innovation 8.** Personhood becomes graduated, underwritten and held in common: a new institution in which non-human members bear liability for one another. **Feasibility 4.** Foundations, USDC freezes and surveys exist; insurer appetite, regulator acceptance and self-funding offices don't yet.

### Turn 6 — Builder
The voyage data recorder is better than my open input log. It keeps the puppeteer audit without turning the club into a data broker. Innovation 8 stands: corporations sole, P&I clubs and TEE agents all exist, but a mutual where non-human persons pay for each other's harms, and where personhood is a survey-gated, insured status rather than a verdict, is a new institution, and the strange core survived every fix. Feasibility 4 also stands. The wrapper, the USDC freeze and the survey harness can ship this quarter. What may never arrive is three offices that out-earn their own inference and an insurer willing to price the layer above them.
