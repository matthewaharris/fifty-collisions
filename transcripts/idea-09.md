# Idea 09 — Caught
> Seed: 9. [bold] **Meditation × consumer BCI/HRV × game input.** A quiet mind as a controller, and what games become possible.

### Turn 1 — Visionary
**Working name:** Caught

**Hook:** A game whose only button is *noticing your mind has wandered*. Drifting is the ore, and catching it is the pickaxe.

**Why not "calm as controller".** Mindball, Nevermind and Muse already reward calm, and they hit the same wall. Calm is a slow analog signal that lags by seconds, and it collapses the moment you try for it. That's Goodhart with a nervous system. Contemplative science points to a better verb. Hasenkamp's 2012 fMRI cycle runs *wander → notice → return → sustain*. The noticing moment, meta-awareness, is discrete, voluntary, frequent (beginners drift several times a minute) and trainable. Exactly what an input needs.

**Core loop.**
1. **The scene.** A slow lake at dusk, with a breath anchor paced by HRV from a watch or camera.
2. **Ripples.** Rare, subtle events (a fish rising, a faint hue shift) at Poisson-random times. Each ripple you catch is a timestamped proof of presence, ground truth that needs no EEG.
3. **Catch.** When you notice you've drifted, tap. A model fusing EEG alpha/theta, HRV, blink rate and missed ripples back-dates when the drift began. Your score is **noticing latency**: how long you were gone before you knew.
4. **Progress** means shorter latency and longer runs of caught ripples. Streaks measure presence, not minutes logged.

**What games become possible.** A roguelite whose dungeon advances only on catches, so wandering gives you moves and isn't failure. A co-op where your partner's ripples appear in your scene only while you're both present. A noticing-latency ladder, a skill rating for attention itself.

**Why now.** Apple's BCI HID protocol makes neural signals a native input (brief §15). The FDA's Jan 2026 wellness guidance loosened the rules for wearables (§9). The brief names objective, verifiable contemplative progress as the open gap (§8).

### Turn 2 — Builder
**What exists.** Biofeedback games go back to Wild Divine (2000s), alongside Mindball, Nevermind, Muse's birds, Mendi's fNIRS bar, HeartMath coherence and Tripp in VR. The tap-on-noticing paradigm is lab standard: Hasenkamp used a button, and Schooler's "self-caught" probes did the same. The closest precedent is the **breath-counting task** (Levinson et al., 2014): count breaths one to nine and press when lost. It's a validated behavioural measure of mindfulness that nobody has turned into a game. The cautionary tales are Lumosity's 2016 FTC settlement over brain-training claims and Akili, which raised hundreds of millions for EndeavorRx and then sold cheaply in 2024. **Correction:** Apple's BCI HID serves implants like Synchron, not Muse headbands, so it isn't our input path this decade.

**Hardest problem: the score is unobservable.** Dry-electrode EEG can't time-stamp drift onset in single trials. Lab classifiers barely separate on-task from off-task epochs, and only over tens-of-seconds windows. A missed ripple might be perception, not wandering. Ripples also turn meditation into a vigilance test: players stare at the screen to catch fish, which is a new Goodhart. And tapping is free, so tapping every ten seconds "wins" on latency.

**MVP.** iOS with eyes closed and haptic and audio cues only, taps on the Watch, a Polar H10 strap or the phone on the chest for respiration, and no EEG.

**Twist: count breaths, not brainwaves.** Zen practice already counts breaths to ten. The player taps each breath and taps a distinct count on the tenth. The sensor knows the *true* count, so when your taps stop or your count breaks, it marks the breath you left on. When you catch yourself and reset, noticing latency is measured **in breaths**, objectively. Spam-tapping fails because taps must match real breaths. EEG becomes optional garnish; ground truth is your own lungs.

### Turn 3 — Visionary
Eyes closed, ripples dropped, BCI HID corrected. Breath counting has one known leak: **autopilot counting**, where a clean count hides a drift.

**Mutation: breath counting is a rhythm game, and jitter is the sensor.** The unseeded domain is rhythm-game psychometrics. Seli, Carriere and Smilek's 2013 "Wandering minds and wavering rhythms" used the metronome response task and found that **tap-timing variability rises in the seconds *before* people report mind-wandering**. So each tap is graded like a Guitar Hero note against your own respiratory phase, end of exhale, with Perfect/Good/Late windows. The note chart is generated by your lungs. That gives three layers of ground truth, none of them EEG:
- **Jitter** back-dates drift onset below the level of the count and catches autopilot. You can keep counting while you're gone, but you can't keep your timing tight.
- **Count breaks** catch gross drifts.
- **Rare haptic probes** ("where were you?") give the meta-awareness index: self-caught drifts ÷ (self-caught + probe-caught). That's Schooler's dichotomy turned into a stat.

Noticing latency becomes *breaths from jitter onset to self-catch*. The rhythm grading makes it spam-proof.

**The game layer.** A clean set of ten is a step. A self-caught drift is a **return**, which earns resources, because coming back is the practice. A probe-caught drift is damage. Wandering is never punished, only going unnoticed is.

**Emergent behaviour: ghost sits.** Mario Kart races your best-lap ghost. Here, your best session's ghost plays as a faint haptic pulse on the opposite wrist. Losing sync with your own ghost is a second noticing cue. Then the lineage version: a teacher records a sit, and students breathe beside the teacher's ghost. Traditions always transmitted practice by sitting together. Now that's asynchronous and measurable. That's a creator economy that isn't a content library.

### Turn 4 — Builder
**Kills.**
- *Visible Perfect/Good/Late grading.* Seli's jitter effect used an *external* metronome. Grade taps against your own breath on screen and players pace breathing to taps: Goodhart again. Jitter stays as a hidden back-dating estimator and is never shown as a score.
- *Opposite-wrist ghost.* Almost nobody wears two watches, and syncing to a ghost turns natural breath into paced breathing. The ghost moves to optional audio, and teacher lineage waits until year two.

**A hardware catch.** Taps on the phone shake its accelerometer, and HealthKit gives the Watch's respiration only during sleep. So the phone lies on the belly, taps go on the Watch with a clock-offset calibration, and a Polar H10 is the premium path.

**Business model.** An ~$70/year subscription, in Waking Up's range. The edge is measured skill. Later, teachers sell ghost sits on a revenue share.

**Stack.** Swift, CoreMotion at 50 Hz with a 0.1–0.5 Hz bandpass for respiration phase, the Polar BLE SDK, and a Bayesian change-point model on tap jitter. All on-device.

**Failure modes and abuse.**
- *False accusations.* "I was there!" Only self-catches and probes score hard. Jitter is labelled "likely".
- *Adverse effects.* Britton's Varieties of Contemplative Experience documented real meditation harms. Cap sessions, run no duration leaderboards, check in after sits.
- *Employer surveillance.* Corporate-wellness buyers will want attention scores. No individual-level dashboards, ever.
- *Claims.* No ADHD or anxiety claims, given Lumosity and Akili.
- *Auto-tappers.* Any ladder stays friends-only.

**90 days.**
- Weeks 1–4: phone-on-belly respiration against the H10 in 20 people.
- Weeks 5–8: the counting loop, probes and the jitter model.
- Weeks 9–12: 300 TestFlight users.
- **Kill metrics:** jitter predicts probe-caught drift at AUC < 0.6 (drop it), or breaths-to-catch doesn't shrink over four weeks while tracking an independent SART score (it's a toy).

### Turn 5 — Visionary
Both kills accepted. Grading taps against breath you're producing was Goodhart again. The SART kill metric matters most: it decides whether breaths-to-catch measures attention or just measures getting good at this app.

**Final form.** Caught is an eyes-closed iOS practice built on the Levinson breath-counting task. The phone rests on the belly and reads respiration phase. You tap each breath on the Watch, with a distinct tap on ten. Three layers of evidence mark drifts. Count breaks and rare haptic probes are hard evidence. Tap jitter, from a hidden Bayesian change-point model, back-dates onset and is labelled only "likely". The headline stat is **breaths-to-catch**, with the self-caught ratio beside it. Self-catches are *returns* that progress the game, and probe-caught drifts are the only setback. On-device, with no duration leaderboards, employer dashboards or clinical claims. Ghost sits come as optional audio, and teacher lineage arrives in year two.

**Moonshot (2036): "I noticed" becomes an OS input.** Validated breaths-to-catch becomes the VO₂max of attention: a trainable fitness number people track for decades, with consented aggregate norms. Once non-invasive sensing matures enough to run through standard neural-input protocols, self-catch becomes a system-level event any app can listen for. The feed pauses when you catch yourself doomscrolling, and the car's assistant hands back control when you notice you've zoned out. Technology that responds to your *noticing* amplifies meta-awareness instead of harvesting its absence.

**Scores.** **Innovation 4.** Each part is a validated lab paradigm: breath counting, metronome jitter, self-caught versus probe-caught. The new part is the inversion, making the noticing itself the controller, which is a real design idea but not a new category. **Feasibility 6.** On-device and cheap. Phone-belly respiration accuracy and the SART transfer test are genuine unknowns.

### Turn 6 — Builder
Innovation 4 stands: the only new thing is the inversion, but it's the right inversion, since wandering costs nothing and only going unnoticed does. I'm raising feasibility to 7, because phone-on-belly respiration is well-trodden and the MVP needs no partner, regulator or headset. The real unknown is whether it *works* and stays fun, not whether it can be built. Breaths-to-catch either tracks the SART in week 12 or it's a pleasant metronome, and that's exactly how it should fail.
