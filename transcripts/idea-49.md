# Idea 49 — Hans
> Seed: 49. [wild] **Interspecies communication × consciousness × ML.** If we can talk to whales, what institutions follow?

### Turn 1 — Visionary
**Working name:** Hans, after the horse that "counted" until Pfungst showed (1907) it was reading its questioner.

**Hook:** Rules of evidence for minds that can only speak through a model. Sperm whales go first, and AI systems are next.

**The isomorphism.** The first institution whale translation needs isn't a treaty but a law of evidence. Every "the whales said X" will arrive through a model trained by people who badly want X to be true. Eleos describes the same trap for AI self-reports, which bend towards what users expect. Whale and AI testimony share one problem: a mind heard only through a model that might be ventriloquising.

**Core mechanic. The verb is *attest*.**
1. **Lexicon by playback.** An ML hypothesis (from CETI-style coda analysis, or DolphinGemma) becomes a lexicon entry only if a preregistered, double-blind playback predicts behaviour beyond chance: a reply coda, an approach, a change in dive. Operators don't know which stimulus is the test: Pfungst's control, built in.
2. **Testimony ledger.** Each attested entry carries an error rate. A statement is admissible only if it is built from attested entries, and its confidence is the product of theirs.
3. **Guardian of record.** On the Whanganui River model, a trustee represents a *clan*, since sperm whale clans have their own dialects. The guardian may cite only attested testimony plus revealed preference: avoidance, blow-sample cortisol, call rates. Ventriloquism disqualifies the guardian.
4. **An honest empty lexicon.** If nothing passes attestation, the guardian has to say "we don't know." That's a finding.

**Why wild.** It creates standing for non-human testimony, with a falsification test as the entry fee. The same court could later hear an AI.

**Why now.** TMC's Clarion-Clipperton mining application was published in August 2026, with a permit expected in Q1 2027. Anthropic, DeepMind and Meta now formally study AI welfare.

### Turn 2 — Builder
**What exists.** Pipelines exist: CETI's coda "phonetic alphabet" (Sharma et al., 2024), Earth Species Project's NatureLM-audio, Google's DolphinGemma. The Coller Dolittle Prize offers $10M for two-way communication. Playback as proof of meaning goes back to the vervet alarm calls of Seyfarth and Cheney (1980). Legal standing has Te Awa Tupua (2017) and the Pacific He Whakaputanga Moana declaration of whale personhood (2024), but the Nonhuman Rights Project lost *Happy* (2022). Birch's evidence framework shaped UK decapod law. Sonotype (idea 34) used decoy playback to *identify* species; Hans asks what they *mean*.

**Hardest problem: the ledger multiplies to zero.** Five attested entries at 0.8 each give a sentence at 0.33. Policy needs "noise here harms us"; the attested lexicon will be approaches and reply codas for a decade, from Dominica clans that don't live in the Clarion-Clipperton Zone. The wild half sits idle, and the guardian quietly becomes ordinary behavioural ecology. Playback also has a cost: synthetic codas injected into a culture that learns socially are an intervention, not an observation.

**Twist: accredit the translator, not the translation.** The near-term danger is motivated ventriloquism, not silence. Miners and NGOs will both commission "what the whales say" before 2027. Hans's first ruling is exclusion: a Daubert standard for non-human testimony. Any pipeline has to pass blind proficiency testing, the way forensic labs do, on two known-answer sets: species whose semantics are already settled (vervet alarms, Japanese tit syntax, dolphin signature whistles), and *planted ventriloquists*, models fine-tuned to produce a desired translation. Fail either, and the pipeline can't testify. The court works from day one, and the same accreditation applies unchanged to AI self-reports.

**First step on Monday.** Build the proficiency benchmark from public vervet, tit and dolphin playback corpora, train five ventriloquist models, and score the existing pipelines against it.

### Turn 3 — Visionary
**Taking accreditation, and refusing to stop there.** Daubert for translators is the right first ruling, but alone it shrinks Hans to a benchmark. Standing has to work with a lexicon of a few entries, and your arithmetic shows how: stop composing sentences.

**Unseeded domain: child testimony.** Courts already faced minds heard through motivated adults. In the McMartin preschool case in the 1980s, suggestive interviewers produced testimony about abuse that never happened. That's ventriloquism with a body count. The fix was the NICHD protocol: no leading questions, and only open prompts. The UN Convention on the Rights of the Child, Article 12, then weighs a child's views "in accordance with age and maturity". And developmental psychologists ask preverbal infants questions with no words at all, using preferential looking and habituation.

Hans borrows all three:
1. **Ask with conditions, not sentences.** A policy question becomes a choice: quiet transect versus a replay of mining noise, with the clan's own attested contact and alarm codas as the readout. Two valence entries at 0.8 answer yes/no with a known error rate. Dominica validates the method; CCZ clans get passive moorings until it transfers.
2. **The non-leading rule.** Playback may use only the clan's own recorded codas, never synthetic ones, and never a stimulus that contains the hypothesised answer. A per-clan playback budget, set the way an IRB sets a dose, limits cultural contamination.
3. **Weight by attested capacity.** A clan's voice in a proceeding grows with its verified lexicon, as a child's does with maturity.

**Second-order effect: a humility premium.** Planted ventriloquists make "unknown" the profitable answer, because pipelines that overclaim fail accreditation. When labs bring AI welfare self-report tools to the same court, they'll face the same planted ventriloquists. Pricing restraint is what lets one court serve every unverifiable mind.

### Turn 4 — Builder
**Kill: playing mining noise to whales.** Asking by inflicting the harm under trial is Level B harassment under the MMPA, and no IRB-style board should approve it. Randomize the quiet instead. Vancouver's ECHO slowdown trials already varied ship speed by period. Hans randomizes voluntary vessel slowdowns by day and reads codas from passive moorings linked to AIS. The non-leading rule holds, and the only intervention is relief.

**Kill: standing weighted by lexicon.** That makes a clan's voice proportional to its research funding, and capacity-weighted standing is the argument disability law spent fifty years dismantling. Every clan gets a guardian. The lexicon weights only the *evidence*, never the standing.

**Failure mode: the humility premium cuts both ways.** Miners love "unknown." If silence reads as no objection, accreditation hands industry the outcome. The rule: unknown testimony moves the burden onto the applicant under the precautionary principle, and the guardian argues from revealed preference.

**Abuse.** Pipelines will train to spot the plants. The accreditor holds a secret, rotating plant set offline and runs it under ISO/IEC 17043 proficiency-testing rules.

**Model.** A nonprofit accreditor: fees from pipeline labs, contracts with regulators, and philanthropy (Coller, Bezos Earth Fund). The durable money arrives when AI labs need their welfare self-report tools accredited.

**Venue.** No new court yet. Hans files as an ISA observer, comments on the NOAA permit for TMC, and feeds IMO's underwater-noise guidelines.

**Stack.** SoundTrap moorings, AIS, CETI-style coda detectors, NatureLM-audio, OSF preregistration.

**90 days.** Days 1–30: benchmark v0 (vervet, Japanese tit and dolphin known-answer sets, plus five public plants and a secret set). Days 31–60: score four pipelines and publish. Days 61–90: preregister the Dominica slowdown protocol and file ISA observer status. **Gate:** the benchmark separates plants from honest pipelines at AUC ≥ 0.9, and one pipeline blindly recovers the vervet alarm classes.

### Turn 5 — Visionary
**Accepting both kills, because both make Hans stranger rather than smaller.** Randomised relief beats my noise playback: the clan answers by how it uses the quiet. Equal standing with weighted evidence is the disability-law lesson applied correctly. The burden shift is the hinge of the whole institution. It turns "we can't hear them" from industry's permission into industry's problem, so a party that can't yet speak still changes who must prove what.

**Final form.** Hans is a nonprofit accreditor and guardianship for testimony from minds heard only through models. Translation pipelines testify only after passing blind proficiency tests under ISO/IEC 17043: known-answer species, plus a rotating secret set of planted ventriloquists. Each clan gets a guardian regardless of its lexicon. The guardian argues from accredited testimony and from randomised relief trials: voluntary vessel slowdowns by day, read through passive moorings linked to AIS. When testimony comes back "unknown", the burden moves to the applicant. Hans starts as an ISA observer and a commenter on TMC's NOAA permit, and it accredits AI welfare self-report tools against the same plants.

**Moonshot (2036).** An Office of Non-Human Testimony sits beside the ISA and the IMO. Clan guardians hold standing in permit proceedings across the Pacific, and their relief trials set seasonal quiet corridors. The first attested two-way exchange happens under the non-leading rule, so nobody can say we put the words in. AI labs' welfare claims, for and against, are heard under the same rules of evidence as a sperm whale's.

**Scores.** Innovation **8**. Rights of nature and bioacoustic ML exist, but forensic accreditation of interspecies and AI testimony, together with a burden shift on silence, is a new institution. Feasibility **4**. The benchmark and slowdown trials are Monday work; standing is a decade-long fight.
