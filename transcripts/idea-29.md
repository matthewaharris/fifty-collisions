# Idea 29 — Order Parameter
> Seed: 29. [wild] **Collective meditation × physiological synchrony × global events.** Rigorous science on whether group coherence is real.

### Turn 1 — Visionary
**Working name:** Order Parameter

**Hook:** Measure humanity's coupling constant, and let "global coherence" live or die on one number: the intercept at zero channel.

**The collision.** Physics has an exact theory of group coherence. Kuramoto oscillators phase-lock once coupling K exceeds a threshold set by the spread of natural frequencies. Meditation shrinks that spread: resonance breathing pulls breath and heart rhythm toward 0.1 Hz. Meditators are the best case for any coupling to show. The Maharishi Effect's "√1% of the population" was a critical-mass claim all along. Kuramoto turns it into a curve you can fit.

**Core loop. The verb is titrate.**
1. **Sit.** Volunteers meditate wearing straps or watches tracking breath and HRV phase.
2. **Assign.** A sealed quantum RNG assigns each session a coupling channel. K0: strangers sit at the same moment and none of them know it. K1: they're *told* others are sitting (belief only). K2: a shared bell. K3: a live audio feed of the group breathing. K4: haptic breath, where you feel a stranger's breath on your wrist.
3. **Measure.** Compute the order parameter r across cohorts of 2 to 100,000. Sham cohorts (same clock times, other sessions) remove circadian artefacts.
4. **Fit.** Fit r(N, K). Known channels give ordinary curves; the nonlocal hypothesis predicts r > sham at K0. Proponents (HeartMath's GCI, Transcendental Meditation researchers) and skeptics preregister their intercepts COGITATE-style.
5. **Global events.** Eclipses and World Cup finals get sealed windows in advance, fixing the Global Consciousness Project's post-hoc selection.

**Honesty.** I expect a zero intercept; the payoff holds anyway. Synchrony through channels is real (Konvalinka 2011: firewalkers' relatives' hearts tracked theirs), and nobody has measured it as a dose-response curve at planetary N.

**Why now.** COGITATE showed adversarial collaborations can wound big theories (§7). Wearables were deregulated in January 2026 (§9). Contemplative tech still lacks objective measures (§8).

### Turn 2 — Builder
**What exists.** The Global Consciousness Project has run random-event generators since 1998. Bancel's 2017 reanalysis concluded that its effect tracked the experimenters' event choices rather than the events. Insight Timer already shows "12,400 meditating now", which is K1 deployed to millions with no measurement. The template that matters is the **Transparent Psi Project** (Kekecs et al., 2023): proponents and skeptics co-designed it, pre-registered, streamed raw data live, ran 2,000+ people, and got a null that both camps accepted in advance.

**Hardest problem: artefactual synchrony.** Resonance breathing pulls everyone to 0.1 Hz, so frequencies cluster by instruction, and a shared start bell resets phases at t=0. Synchrony research is full of this (Palumbo et al., 2017). Without surrogate cohorts (same people, time-shifted) and a fitted decay of start-up alignment, K0 shows "coherence" for free. A null at K0 also means nothing unless the instrument provably detects coupling when coupling is real.

**Twist: the channels are the product, and they're also the positive control.** K4, feeling a stranger breathe on your wrist, is a consumer feature worth selling. Every K3 and K4 session calibrates the instrument; if haptic coupling can't lift r above surrogates, the rig can't judge K0. K0 then becomes a free arm inside a product people pay for, with QRNG assignment of sessions, and the planetary curve fills in as a by-product of a paid app rather than of grants. Proponents must name their minimum population and practice dose before unblinding, so "app users aren't real meditators" is answered in advance.

**MVP.** A Polar H10 (RR intervals give breath phase through RSA) plus a phone app; 1,000 meditators; K0, K1, K3 and K4 randomized; cohorts up to 200; one proponent group and one skeptic group co-signing.
**Kill metric:** K4 fails to beat surrogates.

### Turn 3 — Visionary
**I take the twist, with one refusal.** Haptic co-breathing pays the bills and calibrates the rig. It must not become the identity. If K0 shrinks into a side arm in a wellness app, the idea collapses to bold. The headline stays the intercept.

**Artefacts, killed by design: detuned pacers.** Every K0 meditator gets a secret randomized breathing pace between 5.2 and 6.8 breaths/min and a random start offset. Instructions then produce no alignment; only steady-state windows count. Kuramoto says exactly how much coupling is needed to lock oscillators with a known frequency spread. Any K0 locking against *designed* detuning therefore puts a computable floor under the coupling strength.

**Sensitivity, borrowed from radio astronomy (unseeded).** VLBI finds weak common signals by cross-correlating distant, timestamped stations. Each pair of meditators is a baseline, so 10,000 sitters give 50 million of them. The test also has a signature. Channel artefacts such as shared news, weather or time-of-day have structure in distance and time zone. A nonlocal coupling, as proponents describe it, should be **flat in separation**. A flat excess is the claim. A slope means a leak.

**Second-order effect: a coherence seismograph.** Point the same correlator at passive data from consenting Whoop and Oura users, with no meditation involved, and it measures what really couples us: broadcasts. A penalty shootout, a breaking alert or an election night shows up as a dated jolt of synchrony with a magnitude and a footprint, catalogued the way USGS catalogues quakes. The Maharishi Effect's mundane twin turns out to be real, and the instrument puts a number on it: **media's K**. It measures how hard a feed entrains a nation's heart rhythms. That is a public-health variable nobody tracks, and it brings a new ethics question: whoever can raise K can sell it.

### Turn 4 — Builder
**Refusal accepted:** the intercept stays the headline. **Kills and fixes.**
- *Detuned pacers running all session.* A forced oscillator locks to its pacer, not its neighbours. Use **pace-then-release**: three paced minutes set each person's natural frequency, then silence, and only free-running windows count.
- *50 million baselines.* Pairs share people, so they aren't independent. Inference runs on cohort-level permutation tests against time-shifted surrogates. Timing is easy, since 0.1 Hz needs only 100 ms clock agreement.
- *Seismograph from Oura and Whoop.* Their exports are 5-minute HRV summaries, not beat-to-beat phase. What passive data supports is synchronized *arousal*: heart-rate jolts across a population. That's still real. Cardiac emergencies rose during Germany's 2006 World Cup matches (Wilbert-Lampen, *NEJM* 2008). Call it media's arousal index, not K.

**Integrity.** Raw streams publish live, as in Transparent Psi; QRNG assignments are hash-committed before sessions; analysis code is frozen. K0 unblinds only after the positive control passes. TM groups quietly sitting together "to help" would break blinding, so proponents pre-register whether that counts.

**Ethics.** Haptic strangers stay anonymous and opt in; exclude panic disorder and minors; HRV is special-category health data under GDPR. The arousal index never goes to advertisers or media buyers; the catalogue is aggregated and delayed.

**Sustainability.** Co-breathing subscriptions at about $8 a month carry operations. The BIAL Foundation, which funds both proponents and skeptics, and Templeton fund the adversarial collaboration.

**Stack.** Polar H10 BLE SDK, Apple Watch haptics through WatchKit, NTP-disciplined timestamps, a Kuramoto/phase-locking-value pipeline, and Bayes factors pre-registered at the proponents' predicted effect.

**90 days.** Weeks 1–3: app, protocol, pre-registration. Weeks 4–8: 1,000 meditators, three sits a week. Weeks 9–10: positive control. Weeks 11–12: K0 unblinding, plus one sealed match-night arousal pilot.
**Kill metrics:** K4 fails to beat surrogates, or under 40% retention by week 8.

### Turn 5 — Visionary

Hash-committed QRNG assigns each session a channel: K0 (none), K1 (belief), K3 (audio), K4 (haptic breath). Pace-then-release; only free-running windows count. Analysis fits Kuramoto order parameters against time-shifted surrogates with cohort-level permutation tests. Correlation is plotted against distance: flat excess is the claim, a slope a leak. K0 unblinds only after K4 passes as a positive control. Raw streams are published live, Bayes factors are pre-registered at the proponents' own predicted effect, and both camps co-sign beforehand. Subscriptions fund operations; BIAL and Templeton fund the collaboration. A sealed, delayed **media arousal index** is a side output, never sold. I accept every kill.

**Moonshot (2036).** Order Parameter becomes an **observatory for extraordinary collective claims**. Watches stream beat-to-beat phase by default, and millions of people opt in as standing stations. A claim about global meditation, mass prayer or the Maharishi Effect gets no press until it has *booked observing time*, the way astronomers apply to a telescope. Sealed proposal, predicted effect, minimum population, window, and publication whatever it shows. Its other half produces a planetary map of which channels truly couple us: rituals, choirs, broadcasts, feeds. Co-regulation becomes something you can measure and prescribe for loneliness and panic, alongside sleep and steps.
